What Is the NFL?

The National Football League, or NFL, is the highest level of professional American football in the United States. The league is made up of 32 teams divided between the American Football Conference and the National Football Conference.

NFL games are played between two teams that compete to score more points than their opponent. Teams use offense, defense, and special teams to move the football and prevent their opponent from scoring.

How Football Works

The goal of football is to score more points than the other team. The offense attempts to move the ball down the field while the defense attempts to stop them.

A Typical Offensive Drive

  1. The offense begins with possession of the football.
  2. The quarterback and offense attempt to move the ball.
  3. The team has four downs to gain ten yards.
  4. If the team gains ten yards, it receives another set of downs.
  5. The drive ends when the team scores, turns the ball over, or punts.

Ways to Score

  • Touchdown - 6 points
  • Extra point - 1 point
  • Two-point conversion - 2 points
  • Field goal - 3 points
  • Safety - 2 points

Football Positions

Football players have different positions and responsibilities depending on whether their team is on offense, defense, or special teams.

Common Offensive Positions

  • Quarterback - Throws passes and leads the offense.
  • Running Back - Runs with the football and catches passes.
  • Wide Receiver - Catches passes from the quarterback.
  • Offensive Lineman - Blocks defenders to protect the offense.
  • Tight End - Blocks and catches passes.

Common Defensive Positions

  • Defensive Lineman - Rushes the quarterback and stops runners.
  • Linebacker - Defends against both running and passing plays.
  • Cornerback - Defends wide receivers.
  • Safety - Provides deep defensive coverage.
